CET, Inc. visited the United States Steel Corporation Gary Works facility on April 8, 2026 in order to complete an evaluation of the existing ETL5 Tinning Line in preparation for potential future modifications. Prior to the evaluation, CET reviewed existing and relevant reference documents including drawings, specifications, and equipment. The evaluation completed was general in nature and relied heavily on visual observations made from mill floor, subgrade basements, and elevated platforms located along the ETL5 line.

The evaluation sought to identify the existing condition of several systems present throughout ETL5 including cooling systems, fume exhaust systems, structural systems, etc. As such, the findings in the report below have been organized accordingly. In addition to the systems mentioned, special attention was paid to access routes and material handling paths as well as to identify any potential interferences for potential future equipment.

The following report presents only CET's findings and observations from that field evaluation, no recommendations pertaining to specific deficiencies have been made. The observations and findings presented in this report are based on visual inspection and a review of the provided documentation only. No destructive testing, operational testing, or detailed engineering analysis was performed. Hidden conditions, defects, or deficiencies may exist that were not identifiable during this evaluation.
